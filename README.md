Sum up 25 November's world news in one image: your program writes one image of the day's news, an AI judge compares the images two at a time, and the 50 best win rates share $100: $20 for 1st place, $12 for 2nd, $6 for 3rd, $2 each for 4th to 10th and $1.20 each for 11th to 50th.

A newspaper front page for Wednesday 25 November 2026: one dominant lead — Zelenskyy confirms Ukraine sank a Russian warship off Libya and hit a cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland — with a Houthi drone hitting Riyadh's tallest tower, Trump awarding Rudy Giuliani the Medal of Freedom, and a CIA/ODNI cyber breach as supporting stories, plus a ticker of the day's other news. The image is drawn deterministically by `build_news.py` (no network, no timestamps) and `main.ts` copies the resulting `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
